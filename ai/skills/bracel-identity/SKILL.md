---
name: bracel-identity
description: Change Bracel bearer authentication, token verification, record policies or tenant authorization.
---

Resolve identity capabilities and read their source and the application's policy rules.
Keep issuer/audience/key policy in application configuration. Distinguish route scopes
from record ownership and tenant membership; each protected operation needs the relevant
checks. Exercise missing, expired, invalid and insufficient credentials and cross-owner
access through the HTTP interface. Use disposable credentials and record actual verification.
